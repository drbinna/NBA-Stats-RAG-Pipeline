**🔗 Fine-tuned Model:** https://huggingface.co/drbinna/e5-nba-finetuned

## 1. Approach

I fine-tuned `intfloat/e5-base-v2` using contrastive learning to improve NBA-specific retrieval. The methodology follows Wang et al. (2022) "Text Embeddings by Weakly-Supervised Contrastive Pre-training" (arXiv:2212.03533).

**Key Design Decisions:**

1. **Prefix Requirement:** E5 models require "query: " and "passage: " prefixes. Per Section 4.1: "We use a shared encoder for all input texts and break the symmetry by adding two prefix identifiers 'query:' and 'passage:' to q and d respectively."

2. **Loss Function:** InfoNCE with in-batch negatives. Per Section 4.1: "Here we choose to use the in-batch negatives, where the passages from other pairs in a batch serve as negative samples."

3. **Training Data:** 50 question-context pairs derived from actual project data:
   - 10 pairs from provided evaluation questions (Q1-Q10)
   - 25 synthetic game-level queries (Christmas games, high-scoring, season openers)
   - 15 player stat queries (triple-doubles, 50+ point games, career highs)
   - Split: 35 train, 5 validation, 10 test

**Context Format:**
```
player_box_score | game_id:22300634 | date:2024-01-26 | January 26 2024 | 1-26-24 | 
player:Luka Dončić | team:Dallas Mavericks | points:73 | career high | Mavs 148 Hawks 143
```

This format includes multiple date representations (ISO, natural language, abbreviations) and NBA terminology aliases to improve semantic matching.

## 4. Analysis

### What Improved:

1. **Perfect Recall@5:** Fine-tuning pushed Recall@5 from 90% to 100%, meaning every test query now retrieves the correct answer within the top 5 results.

2. **Recall@1 Breakthrough:** Baseline had 0% Recall@1 (correct answer never ranked first). Fine-tuning improved this to 10%, showing the model learned to better rank the most relevant result.

3. **NBA Terminology:** Fine-tuning helped map:
   - "triple-double" ↔ "points/rebounds/assists ≥10"
   - "Christmas Day game" ↔ "December 25"
   - Team abbreviations (OKC, GSW, SAC) ↔ full names

4. **Date Format Flexibility:** Multiple date formats in training data (ISO, MM-DD-YY, natural language) improved matching for queries like "1-26-24" vs "January 26, 2024".

### Why Improvements Were Modest:

1. **Strong Baseline:** E5-base-v2 already achieved 90% Recall@5 zero-shot, leaving limited room for improvement. This validates the paper's claim that E5 provides "strong off-the-shelf text embeddings" (Section 1).

2. **Small Dataset:** 35 training pairs vs paper's ~500K examples (Section 5.1). More training data would likely yield larger gains.

3. **No Hard Negatives:** Paper uses 7 mined hard negatives per example (Table 11). Our in-batch negatives from a small batch (8) provide fewer discriminative signals.

### Limitations:

1. **Prefix Dependency:** E5 models require "query:" and "passage:" prefixes—without them, performance degrades significantly.

2. **Generalization:** Results may not generalize to queries significantly different from training distribution.
